# Oil Change Car Workshop Management System

The Oil Change Car Workshop Management System is designed to streamline operations for automotive workshops. It supports three types of users—customers, mechanics, and admins/receptionists—while offering functionalities that cater to the needs of each role.

---

## User Types and Functionalities

| User Type        | Key Responsibilities                                    |
|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| **Customer**     | - Register and log in  - Book service appointment  - View service status and history |
| **Mechanic**     | - View assigned jobs  - Update service progress    |
| **Admin**        | - Manage customer records  - Manage appointments  - Generate reports and invoices |

---

## User Requirements Definition

### Customer Requirements

- **Registration and Login:**  
   Customers can create accounts, ensuring secure access to personal information.
  
- **Appointment Booking:**  
   The capability to book appointments tailored to their schedule.
  
- **Service Tracking:**  
   Users can view current service statuses and retrieve their service history.

### Mechanic Requirements

- **Job Assignment View:**  
   Mechanics should have access to a list of jobs assigned to them, facilitating better workload management.

- **Progress Updates:**  
   Mechanics should have the ability to mark jobs as "In Progress," "Completed," or "Pending" with additional comments as necessary.

### Admin Requirements

- **Customer Management:**  
   Admins should be able to add, edit, or delete customer profiles and appointment entries.

- **Reporting and Invoicing:**  
   The system must support the generation of detailed reports and invoices to aid in business operations.

---

## Main Functionalities

1. **Book Service Appointment**  
   Customers can select a date, time, and provide vehicle details for their oil change service. The system should allow for easy navigation and confirmation of appointments.

2. **Update Service Status**  
   Mechanics are responsible for updating the status of services to indicate whether they are "In Progress," "Completed," or if further work is required.

3. **Manage Customer Records**  
   Admins can create, edit, or delete customer and vehicle information, ensuring that all records are current and accurate.

4. **Generate Service Invoice**  
   An invoice is automatically generated after service completion, including all relevant details such as labor, parts used, and total costs.

5. **View Service History**  
   Both admins and customers can access a comprehensive history of previous oil changes, offering transparency and aiding in service decisions.

---

## Considerations for System Development

The workshop management system should also consider:

- **User Authentication:** Ensure secure login procedures for all user roles.
- **Mobile Responsiveness:** A mobile-friendly interface for customers and staff to access the system on the go.
- **Inventory Management:** Integration with inventory systems for parts used during services can enhance operational efficiency.
- **Feedback Mechanism:** Incorporating a system for customer feedback to improve services based on user experience.

This comprehensive structure is intended to enhance overall efficiency, customer satisfaction, and effective management of oil change services, making it a central component of modern workshop operations.
